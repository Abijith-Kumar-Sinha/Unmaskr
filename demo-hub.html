3 · Try these in the popup's “Check any domain” box

pаypal.com
Cyrillic “а” → Likely phishing, 85. Notice there is no “Report as safe” button: a disguised domain can't be whitelisted by a mis-click (RT-08).
https://hdfcbank.com@secure-check.xyz/login
The “@” username trick → Likely phishing, 92: it really goes to secure-check.xyz (RT-01).
paytm-upi.com
Payments brand + a word → Suspicious, 55 (v0.2.0: safe).
paypal.ws
Brand on an obscure country domain → safe, but labelled “Brand name on a country domain”, not “Official” (RT-18).
paypal.com
The real one → Looks safe, “Official domain”. Switch the popup to ML to show the explainable signal bars.
